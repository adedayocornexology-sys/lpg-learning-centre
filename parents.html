Learning styles

“Your child may be learning differently.”

What looks like a lack of focus can be a child trying to understand the world in their own way. Six ways children take information in — and most children are a mix of several.

Visual

Learns by seeing — pictures, diagrams, demonstrations.

Auditory

Learns by hearing — explanations, songs, being read to.

Reading & writing

Learns by words — lists, labels, writing it down.

Kinaesthetic

Learns by doing — hands on it, moving while thinking.

Social

Learns with others — talking it through, working in a group.

Did you know?

Small things that are actually developmental.

💡 Did you know?

Colouring can help children develop fine motor skills. Holding and controlling crayons helps strengthen the muscles in their hands and develop the finger skills they later use for writing.

So when a child spends twenty minutes colouring in, they are not being kept busy. They are building the hand they will write with.
