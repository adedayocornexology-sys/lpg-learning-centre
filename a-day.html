Festac Town, Lagos
A day at LPG

Circle time to home time.

The day has the same shape every day, so children always know what is coming next.

First thing

Arrival & settling

Bags away, names called, a soft start rather than straight into work.

Circle time

The day starts together

Everyone sits, everyone is greeted, and the day ahead is talked through out loud. It is the anchor of the whole timetable.

Morning

Core lessons on the board

Literacy and numeracy on interactive boards, in short focused blocks — a child at the board working through it, not watching someone else do it.

Midday

Break, lunch and outside

Time to run about and eat together. Eating together is part of the teaching.

Afternoon

Lego creativity & preschool projects

Building, testing and rebuilding. This is where hidden talent tends to show itself first.

Later

Experiments & team activities

Hands-on science, sport and group games: coordination, resilience and working with others.

Home time

Handover

A word with whoever collects, about how the day actually went.

Beyond lessons

The year has landmarks.

Trivia Tuesday every week. Father’s Day, when fathers actually come in and take part. Orientation day before term. Live events where children present their own work to a room of parents and neighbours. A school year here is not only terms and tests.
