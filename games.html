Safety

How this is kept safe.

This page shows children’s work, never children.

What is shown about a child?

First name and class only. No photograph, no surname, no age and no contact details.

Can anyone message my child through this?

No. There are no accounts, no comments and no messaging anywhere on this page.

Can we opt out?

Yes, at any time, and the game comes down. Nothing is published without a parent saying yes first.
