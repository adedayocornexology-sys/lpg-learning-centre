Questions

Before you ask.

What ages do you take?

Preschool through Primary, up to Year 4. Age bands shift slightly by room, so they are confirmed on a visit.

How big are the classes?

Deliberately small, so every child is known by name rather than by average.

What does it cost?

Fees are discussed on a visit, so you get the figure for your child’s stage rather than a range that turns out not to apply.

Are the classrooms air-conditioned?

Yes. Fully air-conditioned, secure spaces are part of what LPG calls world-class comfort.

Do you use technology in lessons?

Every day — interactive boards and digital tools, used to teach rather than to impress visitors.

Come and see us.

Tell us when suits and we will have someone show you round. No commitment — most parents visit twice before deciding.
