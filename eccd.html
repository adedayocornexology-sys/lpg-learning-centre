Early Childhood Care & Development.

LPG Learning Centre, in partnership with the Federal Ministry of Education and the World Bank under the IDEAS-TVET initiative, invites applications from eligible Nigerian youths for a free six-month skills acquisition programme in Early Childhood Care and Development.

It is designed to equip you with practical skills and nationally recognised competencies for work in nurseries, preschools, daycare centres, NGOs and other early childhood learning environments.
