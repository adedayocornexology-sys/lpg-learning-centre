# What she actually asked for

Source: Aunty Funke's phone call to the founder, **6 September 2026**. She walked him
through their existing dashboard, handed over login access, and described what she
wants. Recorded here on 7 September from his account of the call.

This supersedes guesswork. `CLIENT_BRIEF_2026-09-07.md` decodes his written note from
the same period; where the two differ, **this file is what she said out loud**.

---

## Parents

- **Subscribe to reports on their child** at a chosen frequency: daily, weekly or
  monthly.
- View the child's report.
- **Send feedback and complaints** to the school.
- Message the school directly, so it goes both ways.
- **Pay school fees online.**
- **A PTA section**, possibly including video calls.
- Reach the child's page on the child's behalf. **No student accounts** — they are
  minors. This confirms the decision already in `ONTOLOGY.md` §5.

> His own note on the reports: make it **free for now**, and consider charging for it
> later. Worth a flag when that day comes — charging a parent to see their own child's
> report reads differently from charging for a premium service, and a school gets one
> chance to get that wrong. Free now is the right call.

## Teachers — the part she stressed hardest

> **Teachers do not use the system they have. She wants teacher use to become a
> culture.**

- Find out **why** they do not engage. (Partly answered already, below.)
- The interface must feel **familiar**. Most of the teachers are women who live in
  WhatsApp and email, so the teacher dashboard should feel like that, not like being
  handed new software to learn.
- Give them **the tools they actually need to do the job**: curriculum, lesson notes,
  and **templates and formats**, so that opening it is not "another learning
  experience".

### Why they don't engage — found on their live system, 7 Sept 2026

This is evidence, not opinion. Every item was seen on the signed-in dashboard:

- **The Teachers Note screen has no Create button.** A teacher literally cannot write
  a note from the screen named after them. Only Edit and View on notes that already
  exist.
- Its panel header is mislabelled **"Messages"**, copied from another screen.
- **Every icon renders as an empty box** — the icon font never loads, on every page.
- The sidebar greets you with **"Welcome,"** and no name; the top right says the
  literal word **"Name"**.
- **`SubmittedBy` is blank on every row**, so no teacher's work is ever attributed to
  them.
- Newest pupil record: **March 2025**. Total transactions: **0**.

A teacher gains nothing by logging in, and cannot do the one thing the system names
after her. That is the answer to her question, and it is worth telling her plainly.

## Students

- A student profile exists as a record.
- **No student accounts, ever.** Parents access it for them.

## Admin — framed as "the principal"

- **Broadcast information** to parents, teachers and staff.
- Strong control: *"every function of the principal"*.
- **Assign each person's rights.** Permissions are hers to grant.
- Roles must be **well defined**.

## The direction he wants this to grow into

> Every role eventually has an agent — a student agent, a teacher agent, a parent
> agent, or one agent that behaves according to whoever it is serving.

That is the harness thesis in `HARNESS.md`, and it is why the action layer and the
role model get built properly the first time. Not now, but do not build in a way that
forecloses it.

## Integration

All of this **integrates into the eleven-page site already built**, rather than being
a separate application.

## Honest read on size

This is a **school management platform**, not a dashboard tidy-up. Online fee payment
is a Paystack integration on its own. Reports, subscriptions, messaging, a PTA area
with video, a teacher toolkit with templates, and a permissions system for a principal
are each real pieces of work.

**It is comparable to, or larger than, the signed Ajasin Foundation contract**
(₦1,500,000, paid 50/25/25). The ₦400,000–₦600,000 previously floated was priced
against *a website* and badly undersells this.
