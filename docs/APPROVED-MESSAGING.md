# LPG Learning Centre — approved school messaging

Source: write-up supplied by the school (Aunty Funke), 8 November 2026.
The passages below are preserved verbatim. Use the mission and vision as approved statements; do not paraphrase them when presenting them as the school's official mission or vision.

## Core positioning

Welcome to LPG Learning Centre.
The future ready school

To carve a distinctive niche recognised for fostering a dynamic and innovative environment that empowers students with a robust foundation in technology and a holistic approach to education.

At the heart of our mission is the belief that education goes beyond textbooks and classrooms. We are committed to providing a comprehensive education that not only equips students with technical prowess but also nurtures their creativity, communication skills and character.

Our goal is to produce well rounded individuals who are not only competent in their chosen fields but also possess the resilience and adaptability needed to thrive in an ever-evolving world.

We shall remain steadfast in our pursuit of excellence, combining technical proficiency with a holistic educational approach, to shape the future leaders and innovators of tomorrow

## Our Mission

> To raise adaptable learners — grounded in technology, rich in creativity and strong in character — ready to thrive in an ever-changing world.

## Our Vision

> To be recognised as a distinctive, dynamic and innovative learning centre shaping confident future leaders and innovators of tomorrow.
